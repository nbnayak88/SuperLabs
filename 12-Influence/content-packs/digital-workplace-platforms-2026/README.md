### Proof-of-concept acceptance tests

- POC-01 Employee sees only authorized apps, content and tasks.
- POC-02 Manager sees role-appropriate employee services without unauthorized data.
- POC-03 Onboarding journey shows assigned steps and completion status.
- POC-04 SuccessFactors integration handles a simulated employee transfer correctly.
- POC-05 Enterprise search respects permissions and identifies source documents.
- POC-06 Mobile experience and accessibility are tested.
- POC-07 Targeted communication reaches the correct eligible audience.
- POC-08 SSO, identity provisioning and audit logs are evidenced.
- POC-09 Integration failure and recovery are demonstrable.
- POC-10 Vendor supplies itemized 5-year TCO and exit/export approach.

## Discovery questions

1. Is the primary need an HR portal, enterprise intranet, IT/HR service center, or cross-enterprise digital workplace?
2. Which employee personas, worker types, countries, languages and devices are in scope?
3. Which SAP SuccessFactors modules and SAP BTP services are already licensed?
4. Which journeys require orchestration and which are only navigation?
5. What must remain in SuccessFactors versus be surfaced in the workplace layer?
6. What integrations are required with Microsoft 365, ServiceNow and non-SAP systems?
7. What are the requirements for Joule/AI, enterprise search and knowledge governance?
8. What are the security, accessibility, data residency and content ownership constraints?
9. What outcomes define success: task completion, time saved, adoption, satisfaction?
10. What implementation budget, timeline and support model are expected?

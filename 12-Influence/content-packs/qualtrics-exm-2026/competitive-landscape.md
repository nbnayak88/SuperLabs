## Market intelligence caveats

- “Gartner 2026 Top 10” is not a substantiated published ranking; do not attribute this table to Gartner.
- Gartner Peer Insights ratings depend on product category, review volumes and retrieval date. Do not treat ratings as analyst capability scores.
- Qualtrics ≠ SAP subsidiary (following 2023 divestment).
- Functionality, feature packaging, connectors, localization and licensing may change: confirm against current vendor materials.
- Benchmarking must be validated for industry, country, survey, respondent set, sample size, statistical methodology and permitted data use.

## Competitive questions to ask

1. Which EC hire/transfer/termination events initiate surveys without manual work?
2. Is there a maintained, certified SAP SuccessFactors connector? What fields/entities, delta strategy, API limits and monitoring?
3. How are manager hierarchies and effective-dated organizational changes processed?
4. What respondent minimum and suppression rules are enforced for all dashboard cuts?
5. Can free-text verbatims be masked/redacted before AI processing?
6. Where are data processed/stored and which sub-processors and foundation models are involved?
7. Are benchmarking cohorts comparable and available contractually?
8. How do insights produce accountable actions and later effectiveness measurement?
9. Which GCC languages, accessibility standards and mobile survey channels are supported?
10. What are the real TCO, implementation effort, change management and exit/export options?

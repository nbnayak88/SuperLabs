## Decision gates

- **Gate 1: Fit:** all must-have requirements accepted, including privacy and integration.
- **Gate 2: Proof:** demonstrate same scenarios with masked/synthetic data; record scores and evidence.
- **Gate 3: Architecture:** validate implementation topology, security, data flows, ownership and support.
- **Gate 4: Commercial:** five-year TCO with setup, connectors, licensing tiers, AI usage, regional needs and exit.
- **Gate 5: Selection:** weighted score + written exceptions + approval from EX, HRIS, infosec and procurement.

## Presales deliverables

- Approved scope statement and stakeholder map
- Enterprise EXM capability map and integration diagram
- Data privacy and AI risk review
- Requirement traceability matrix and evidence-based RFI
- Scripted POC plus scored outcomes and commercial model

**Workshop:** 90-minute cross-functional discovery with HR/EX sponsor, HRIS/EC owner, integration architect, privacy, security, analytics and procurement.

# C18 — 100-Minute Role-Based Demonstration

**Personas:** payroll manager, employee, HR master-data lead, time administrator, country tax specialist, finance controller, integration architect and security reviewer.

| Minutes | Scenario | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | SAP-first landscape and country scope | Engine vs orchestration boundary |
| 10–22 | EC employee replication and wage types | Effective dates and mapping |
| 22–34 | Gross-to-net and statutory calculation | Payroll results and rules |
| 34–46 | Time, overtime and benefits deductions | Input reconciliation |
| 46–58 | Retro, off-cycle and final settlement | Corrections and audit |
| 58–70 | Payroll controls and bank payments | Approvals and file integrity |
| 70–80 | Finance posting and statutory reports | GL reconciliation |
| 80–88 | Privacy, fraud and negative tests | Segregation of duties |
| 88–96 | Failed integration replay and payslips | Recovery and employee UX |
| 96–100 | Five-year TCO and selection | Country-specific assumptions |

Challenge cases: retro pay correction, unauthorized payment file, incorrect deduction, employee transfer, missing time input, rejected bank file and duplicate employee event.

Demo scoring: payroll calculation 30%; compliance 20%; SAP/finance integration 20%; controls/privacy 20%; commercial 10%.

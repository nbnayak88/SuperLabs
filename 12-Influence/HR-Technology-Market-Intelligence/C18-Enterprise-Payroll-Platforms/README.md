## Business outcomes
Accurate and timely gross-to-net payroll, country compliance, transparent employee pay, controlled payments, finance reconciliation and auditable payroll operations.

## Boundaries
C18 evaluates enterprise payroll engines. C19 evaluates global payroll orchestration across providers; C20 evaluates statutory reporting/compliance specialist capabilities. Do not treat payroll engine, orchestration hub and employer-of-record service as interchangeable.

## Mandatory principle
Verify each country's statutory localization and vendor service scope independently. Sensitive payroll data requires least-privilege access, controlled payments and human approval.

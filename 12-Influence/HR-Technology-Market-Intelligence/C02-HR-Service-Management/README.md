## Scope boundary
C02 owns HR service requests, case classification, routing, knowledge, agent experience, SLA, HR support analytics and case-related automation. C03 Work Zone is a possible employee front door, **not** a substitute for the full case-management capability. C27 VoE/EXM measures employee sentiment, not case resolution. C29 covers broader workflow automation.

## Product lifecycle caution
SAP SuccessFactors Enterprise Service Management supersedes Employee Central Service Center in the user interface when enabled; migration, licensing and coexistence must be confirmed. Do not represent Work Zone alone as SAP's HR case management engine.

## Core KPIs
Contact rate per 100 employees; self-service containment; first-contact resolution; median time to resolve; SLA compliance; reopen rate; knowledge helpfulness; backlog age; sensitive-case leakage incidents; employee service satisfaction; agent effort.

## Governance
Do not infer analyst rankings from product listings. Use a synthetic workforce for demonstrations; protect employee relations, medical, disciplinary and payroll cases with separate access policies. Every vendor score remains TBD until evidenced.

# C02 — Thirty-Question Presales Discovery

Record: Answer | Current system | Country | Priority | Evidence | Owner | Gap | Decision.

| ID | Domain | Question |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | Outcomes | What employee-service problems and measurable targets drive the initiative? |
| D02 | Demand | What monthly HR case volumes exist by category, geography and channel? |
| D03 | Scope | Which employee, contingent, alumni and manager populations need service? |
| D04 | Model | Is HR support centralized, tiered, shared-service or federated? |
| D05 | Catalog | Which L1/L2/L3 service categories and request forms are required? |
| D06 | Channels | Which channels—portal, email, Teams, phone, chat—must create or update cases? |
| D07 | Intake | What mandatory fields, attachments and consent are needed by category? |
| D08 | Routing | How do region, language, legal entity, priority and expertise determine routing? |
| D09 | SLA | What response/resolution SLAs, business calendars and pause rules apply? |
| D10 | Escalation | How are breached, aged, high-risk and VIP cases escalated? |
| D11 | Knowledge | Where are policies stored, approved, translated and retired? |
| D12 | Deflection | What percentage of contacts should be solved without creating a case? |
| D13 | Agent | What case desktop, templates, bulk actions and collaboration are required? |
| D14 | Security | Who may access confidential HR and employee-relations cases? |
| D15 | Privacy | What residency, retention, legal hold and deletion rules apply? |
| D16 | HR data | Which EC person, employment, manager and organization attributes are needed? |
| D17 | History | How must future-dated and transferred employees be represented in cases? |
| D18 | Integration | Which HCM, payroll, benefits, IAM, document and ITSM systems connect? |
| D19 | Integration | What interface latency, retry, deduplication and monitoring are mandatory? |
| D20 | AI | Which AI tasks may be automated versus human-approved? |
| D21 | AI | What source attribution, multilingual and hallucination tests are required? |
| D22 | Experience | Which employee journeys need guided service rather than ticket forms? |
| D23 | Access | What mobile, accessibility, SSO and frontline requirements exist? |
| D24 | Analytics | Which KPIs are required by CHRO, service owner, agent and country lead? |
| D25 | Quality | How are CSAT, reopen rate and resolution quality assessed? |
| D26 | Legacy | Is Employee Central Service Center or another case tool currently used? |
| D27 | Migration | Which cases, attachments, knowledge and audit history must migrate? |
| D28 | Delivery | What countries, waves, support model and change plan are expected? |
| D29 | Commercial | What license, AI usage, support and five-year cost constraints exist? |
| D30 | Success | What mandatory POC tests and go/no-go thresholds will decide selection? |

## Workshop outputs
Signed service catalog, operating model, channel inventory, HR data map, security classification, knowledge governance, integration diagram, KPI baseline, RFI scope and synthetic POC dataset.

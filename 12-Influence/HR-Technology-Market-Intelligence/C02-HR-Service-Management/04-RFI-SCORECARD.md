## Mandatory gates
Sensitive case privacy, least-privilege access, auditable case lifecycle, correct employee identity, no silent case loss, defensible AI handling, legal retention and workable SLA reporting.

## Commercial normalization
Compare HR agent and employee license units, implementation, integrations, migration, premium AI usage, environments, support, localization, knowledge migration, training, operational staffing and exit costs over five years.

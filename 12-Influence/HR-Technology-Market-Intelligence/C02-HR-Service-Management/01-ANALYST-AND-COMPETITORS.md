# C02 — Analyst Evidence and Top Ten Evaluation Platforms

## Gartner primary market
**Integrated HR Service Management Solutions (IHRSM)** is the closest exact Gartner market label. Its mandatory capabilities include knowledge bases; employee/manager delivery via portal or virtual assistant; and case ticketing, routing and SLA monitoring.

Source: https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/integrated-hr-service-management-solutions

Gartner also evaluates Employee Experience/IHRSM as a use case in **Critical Capabilities for HCM Suites for 1,000+ Employee Enterprises**, published 31 August 2026:
https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/8326817

**IDC and Forrester:** HR service delivery overlaps broader HCM, employee experience, and service-management research. No dedicated 2026 IDC MarketScape or Forrester Wave matching this exact Gartner market is asserted without source verification. Do not conflate intranet or IT service management rankings with HR case-management rankings.

## Competitive positioning hypotheses
- SAP: Employee Central context and SAP HCM-centered service experience may reduce data duplication; validate real architecture, licensing and limitations.
- ServiceNow: Mature cross-function service operations; validate HR privacy segmentation and integration cost.
- Oracle: Strong Oracle ecosystem alignment; test non-Oracle HCM scenarios.
- Leena AI: Conversational resolution potential; validate agent escalation, audit and source reliability.
- HR Acuity: Specialist sensitive employee-relations processes; not automatically a replacement for broad HR service management.

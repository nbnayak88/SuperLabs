## Business outcomes
Reliable compliance, role-based upskilling, training administration, instructor-led and digital delivery, certification tracking, auditable transcripts and measurable learning impact.

## Category boundary
C15 is the enterprise learning system of record and administration. C16 is learning experience/discovery. C17 is skills intelligence. C11 is leadership coaching. Do not treat content marketplaces, LXP or coaching platforms as automatically equivalent to a regulated LMS.

## Evidence policy
Validate exact content standards, external learning, AI functionality and integration by product version, licensing and implementation. No unsupported analyst rankings.

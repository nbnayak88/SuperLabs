**Personas:** learner, manager, LMS administrator, compliance officer, instructor, content creator, HR architect and security reviewer.

| Minutes | Scenario | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Learning operating model and SAP architecture | SKU, source systems, population |
| 10–22 | Job-based mandatory assignment | Effective-dated identity and rules |
| 22–34 | Curriculum and prerequisites | Enrollment and due dates |
| 34–46 | SCORM/content playback and assessment | Completion and score evidence |
| 46–58 | Instructor-led session and waitlist | Attendance and resources |
| 58–70 | Certification renewal and compliance audit | Expiration, history and report |
| 70–80 | Skills and development pathway | Evidence and learning links |
| 80–88 | AI assistance, privacy and access | Human review and negative tests |
| 88–96 | API failure, reconciliation and migration | No duplicate/lost completions |
| 96–100 | Five-year TCO and selection | Commercial assumptions |

## Live challenges
Employee transfers country; certification expires; course revision changes; manager sees another legal entity; content sends conflicting completion status; external learner enters catalog; AI suggests invalid compliance training.

Demo scoring: compliance 30%; learning operations 20%; interoperability 20%; security/analytics 20%; UX/TCO 10%.

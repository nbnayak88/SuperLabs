## Suggested acceptance
100% mandatory compliance, permissions, transcript and certification scenarios pass; zero silent completion loss or duplicate award; 100% integration failures detected and safely replayed; ≥95% noncritical workflows without workaround; content standard support verified with actual packages.

## Selection gates
G1 discovery → G2 learning/compliance architecture → G3 vendor demo → G4 POC and weighted RFI → G5 five-year TCO → G6 executive selection.

## Decision record
Learning outcomes | SAP-first architecture | shortlist | analyst evidence limits | RFI | POC | audit and content standards | migration | TCO | recommendation | approvals.

## SuccessLabs experiential lab
A fictional industrial company must certify plant operators annually. Learners act as compliance lead, LMS admin, instructor, worker and auditor; they diagnose a failed SCORM completion, repair a certification record and defend an audit trail.

# C05 — 100-Minute Presales Demonstration

**Personas:** TA director, recruitment marketer, recruiter, passive candidate, hiring manager, privacy officer, integration architect and procurement.

| Minutes | Scenario | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 | Business goals and SAP portfolio options | Architecture, SKUs, constraints |
| 8–20 | Multilingual career landing page | Brand, mobile, SEO, accessibility |
| 20–30 | Event lead capture and consent | Form, consent proof, source |
| 30–42 | Segment prospects into talent pools | Explainable rules and deduplication |
| 42–55 | Build and approve nurture campaign | Templates, targeting, human approval |
| 55–65 | Multi-channel engagement and opt-out | Email/SMS where supported, suppression |
| 65–75 | AI-assisted personalization | Human editing, guardrails, audit |
| 75–85 | Convert lead to ATS applicant | Identity and source attribution |
| 85–93 | Recruitment marketing analytics | Funnel and campaign ROI |
| 93–100 | Security, error replay, TCO and migration | Negative tests and written assumptions |

## Challenge scenarios
1. Candidate opts out between scheduling and campaign send; suppression must win.
2. Duplicate lead from career site and campus event; show provenance and merge.
3. Recruiter tries to export restricted pool; deny and audit.
4. AI suggests targeting based on protected characteristic; block/review.
5. Candidate applies months later; preserve lawful source attribution.
6. Country-specific retention expires; remove or anonymize as required.
7. ATS API is unavailable; retry safely without duplicate application.
8. Compare legacy SF Recruiting Candidate Experience with SmartRecruiters path using same scenario.

## Demo scoring
Campaign and CRM depth 30%; privacy and security 25%; integration/analytics 20%; candidate UX 15%; operational credibility 10%.

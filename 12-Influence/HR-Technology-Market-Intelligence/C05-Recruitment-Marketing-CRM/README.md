## Taxonomy boundary
C05 owns employer-brand career experiences, candidate lead capture, talent communities/pools, segmentation, nurture journeys, communication, recruitment marketing analytics and conversion. C04 owns the end-to-end ATS/requisition-to-offer process. C06 owns specialist assessment/interview intelligence. Test integrations without duplicating category IDs.

## SAP portfolio guardrail
SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting Candidate Experience comprises Career Site Builder, CRM and Advanced Analytics within the broader Recruiting product. SAP states these elements are no longer sold as independent standalone products. SmartRecruiters for SAP SuccessFactors is an additional SAP portfolio path; confirm migration, coexistence, commercial terms and exact feature entitlements. Do not position SmartRecruiters as an external competitor.

## Human-led AI
AI can recommend segments, personalize draft outreach and surface prospects. Human recruiters approve audience rules, campaigns, candidate communications and any hiring decision. Consent, fairness, explainability and opt-out are mandatory.

## KPIs
Visitor-to-lead conversion; lead-to-applicant conversion; applicant-to-hire conversion; consent capture; talent-pool freshness; campaign open/click/reply; qualified pipeline by critical role; cost per qualified lead; source-to-hire attribution; time-to-engage; unsubscribe and complaint rate.

## Evidence standard
Competitor longlist is curated, not an analyst ranking. Product claims, licensing, data residency and AI functionality require vendor-specific proof.

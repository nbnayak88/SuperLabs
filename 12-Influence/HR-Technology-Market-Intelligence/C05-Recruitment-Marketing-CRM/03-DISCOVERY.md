# C05 Discovery Workshop — 35 Questions

Capture answer, evidence, region, priority, accountable owner and follow-up.

| ID | Theme | Question |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | Strategy | Which critical roles need proactive talent pipelines? |
| D02 | Strategy | What business outcomes and hiring targets define success? |
| D03 | Geography | Which countries, languages, brands and legal entities are in scope? |
| D04 | Volume | What are monthly site visits, leads, applications and hires? |
| D05 | Audience | Which passive, active, alumni, internal and campus segments matter? |
| D06 | Brand | How many career sites and employer-brand variants are needed? |
| D07 | Brand | What SEO, accessibility, mobile and content approval rules apply? |
| D08 | Channels | Which paid, organic, social, events and referral channels are used? |
| D09 | Attribution | How are source codes, UTMs and attribution windows defined? |
| D10 | Lead capture | Which forms and candidate attributes are truly necessary? |
| D11 | Consent | Which lawful bases and opt-in rules apply per channel/country? |
| D12 | Privacy | What notice, retention, deletion and portability rules apply? |
| D13 | Identity | How are duplicates, reapplications and multiple profiles reconciled? |
| D14 | Pools | How are talent pools created, owned, maintained and retired? |
| D15 | Segmentation | Which job family, skill, location and interest attributes are allowed? |
| D16 | Campaigns | Which nurture journeys are currently operating? |
| D17 | Campaigns | What email/SMS/WhatsApp and frequency limits are required? |
| D18 | Templates | Who approves branded content, translation and legal disclaimers? |
| D19 | Engagement | What response, bounce, unsubscribe and complaint targets exist? |
| D20 | Personalization | Which content rules and recommendations may be automated? |
| D21 | AI | What human review, transparency and bias controls are required? |
| D22 | Events | How are campus, webinar and talent event attendees captured? |
| D23 | Referrals | How do employee referrals join CRM and ATS without duplicates? |
| D24 | ATS | Which ATS is authoritative for applications and status? |
| D25 | Handoff | What events convert prospect to applicant and to hire? |
| D26 | SAP path | Is legacy SAP Recruiting, SmartRecruiters or another ATS deployed? |
| D27 | Integration | Which APIs, identity systems and campaign tools connect? |
| D28 | Reliability | How are retries, consent sync, replay and failures monitored? |
| D29 | Analytics | How are visitor-to-lead and lead-to-hire conversion measured? |
| D30 | Analytics | How are campaign ROI and source-to-hire quality defined? |
| D31 | Security | Who may export prospect data or access restricted pools? |
| D32 | Migration | What legacy profiles, consent proofs and campaign histories move? |
| D33 | Operations | Who owns content, campaigns, talent communities and data quality? |
| D34 | Commercial | What five-year licenses, ad spend, AI, services and support budget exists? |
| D35 | Decision | What POC tests and evidence thresholds will decide the platform? |

## Outputs
Talent audience map, journey maps, source taxonomy, consent matrix, campaign catalog, CRM/ATS integration map, baseline funnel, KPI dictionary, migration backlog, SAP portfolio decision and POC dataset.

# C05 Market, Analyst Evidence and Competitors

## Market definition
Recruitment marketing attracts prospective candidates through employer branding, career sites, campaigns and channel attribution. Candidate relationship management organizes talent pools and nurtures consented prospects through personalized, measurable communications before and between applications.

## SAP-first competitive hypotheses
- SAP legacy path: demonstrate Career Site Builder, lead capture forms, talent pools, email campaigns and Advanced Analytics end to end.
- SmartRecruiters path: demonstrate multichannel engagement, nurture, CRM-to-ATS and SAP HCM context; request explicit migration/entitlement plan.
- Avature: test complex segments, event-triggered campaigns and recruiter autonomy.
- Phenom: test personalized career journeys and AI recommendations.
- Beamery/Eightfold: test skills-led discovery and explainable matching.
- Radancy/Symphony Talent: test employer-brand and paid media attribution.
- Gem/Clinch: test prospecting productivity, compliance and source-to-hire measurement.

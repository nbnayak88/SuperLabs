# 100-minute role-based demonstration

Personas: CHRO, CFO, business strategist, workforce planner, HRBP, skills lead and SAP architect.

| Minutes | Scenario | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Strategy and planning scope | Decision ownership |
| 10–22 | EC baseline and finance data | Reconciliation |
| 22–34 | Demand driver model | Formula trace |
| 34–46 | Supply and attrition | Roll-forward |
| 46–58 | Skills and capacity gaps | Gap evidence |
| 58–70 | Growth and downturn scenarios | Version isolation |
| 70–80 | Hire/train/redeploy and cost | Trade-offs |
| 80–88 | Privacy and approval | Negative tests |
| 88–96 | Actuals refresh and recovery | Variance and replay |
| 96–100 | TCO and selection | Decision |

Score modeling 30%, interventions 20%, finance/SAP integration 25%, governance 15%, TCO 10%. Challenge: growth target rises while hiring freezes and training lead times increase; compare feasible alternatives.

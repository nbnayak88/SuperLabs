# C13 — 100-Minute Role-Based Demonstration

**Personas:** employee, dependent, benefits administrator, HRBP, payroll lead, carrier integration analyst, privacy officer and SAP architect.

| Minutes | Scene | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Country scope and SAP-first architecture | Localization and provider boundaries |
| 10–22 | Configure plan and eligibility | Country, job, employee and dependent rules |
| 22–34 | Open enrollment and employee election | UX, validation, accessibility |
| 34–46 | Life event and dependent verification | Effective dates, audit |
| 46–58 | Contributions and flexible benefit credit | Calculation and cost split |
| 58–70 | Carrier/TPA integration | Acknowledgment, retries, duplicates |
| 70–80 | Payroll deduction and retro reconciliation | Code mapping, totals |
| 80–88 | Privacy and AI-guidance negative tests | Restricted data and escalation |
| 88–96 | Analytics, support and renewal | Cost/participation, exceptions |
| 96–100 | Commercial and implementation review | Five-year TCO |

## Live challenges
Employee becomes eligible midyear; dependent documentation missing; plan enrollment expires; provider rejects enrollment; payroll receives duplicate deduction; employee transfers legal entity; AI incorrectly promises coverage and must be corrected.

Demo scoring: country/eligibility 30%; provider/payroll integration 25%; privacy/compliance 20%; employee experience 15%; operations/TCO 10%.

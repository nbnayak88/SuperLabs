## Business outcome
Deliver accurate, compliant and understandable benefits eligibility, enrollment, contribution calculation, provider connectivity and payroll reconciliation across countries.

## Category boundaries
C13 covers benefits administration, enrollment, eligibility, life events and carrier/TPA integration. C12 covers compensation planning; C14 covers recognition; C18–C20 cover payroll execution and statutory reporting.

## Architectural principle
Never presume uniform SAP benefits localization across all countries. Build a country-by-country fit-gap matrix and distinguish SAP-native, partner-enabled, external benefits administrator and manual/legal process paths.

## Governance
Benefits can include highly sensitive personal information. Minimize data, restrict dependent and health-related records, apply country-specific legal review, and preserve human access to benefits specialists for consequential decisions.

# C13 — Market and Analyst Evidence

## Market segmentation
1. Core benefits administration: eligibility, plan setup, enrollment, life events, dependent management and contributions.
2. Benefits experience: decision support, communications, flexible benefits and employee self-service.
3. Outsourced benefits administration: carriers, TPAs, brokers, service centers, compliance and managed operations.
4. Benefits connectivity: carrier feeds, payroll deductions, pensions and statutory provider reporting.

**Personas:** operations director, workforce planner, site supervisor, frontline employee, HR labor compliance lead, payroll lead and SAP integration architect.

| Minutes | Scenario | Required proof |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | SAP-first scheduling landscape | C21/C22/C23 boundary |

## Live challenge
An industrial plant faces a 20% demand surge while a certified technician is absent. Demonstrate safe replacement, fair overtime distribution, schedule publication, manager override and payroll impact.

Score: demand/optimization 30%, compliance/skills 25%, SAP integration 20%, worker UX/fairness 15%, TCO 10%.

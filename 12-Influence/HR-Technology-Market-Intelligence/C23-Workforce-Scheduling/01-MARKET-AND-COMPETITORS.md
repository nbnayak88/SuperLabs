## Market comparison cautions
Some providers sell complete WFM suites; others specialize in frontline shift scheduling. Compare exact SKU and geography, not marketing-level claims. SAP integration may be native, partner-supported or custom. Require a current supported architecture and evidence from comparable customer deployments.

## Outcomes
Forecast staffing demand, create compliant skills-based rosters, reduce understaffing and avoidable overtime, manage disruptions, enable fair shift changes and connect schedules to time/payroll.

## Taxonomy boundaries
C21 covers enterprise workforce management; C22 covers time capture and valuation; **C23 owns forecasting, scheduling, optimization and roster publication**. Do not imply SAP SuccessFactors Time Tracking alone is a full advanced scheduling optimizer.

## Architecture principle
Labor laws and collective agreements are hard constraints; worker privacy, fairness and human override are mandatory evaluation gates. Vendor capabilities and country coverage require validation.

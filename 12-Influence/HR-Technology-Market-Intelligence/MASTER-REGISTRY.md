## Classification cautions

- Competitor entries are candidate platforms, not an analyst-ranked list and not necessarily feature-equivalent products.
- For C02, C06, C11, C14, C16, C19, C23, C26 and similar specialist categories, SAP's offering may rely on integrations or ecosystem partners. Verify fit before describing it as a native SAP product.
- C03 is a cross-stream category shared by HR01 and HR10; C27 is anchored to HR10. C17 and C29 are also shared categories.
- Research boundaries between adjacent categories overlap; this taxonomy intentionally assigns a stable category ID to each capability market for presales reuse.

# C09 — 100-Minute Role-Based Demonstration

**Personas:** CHRO, succession lead, HRBP, line manager, employee, talent reviewer, SAP architect and security reviewer.

| Minutes | Scene | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Business continuity and architecture | SAP scope, prerequisites and licensing |
| 10–22 | Identify critical positions in Succession Org Chart | Position/incumbent context |
| 22–34 | Talent Search, pool and nominations | Readiness and approval |
| 34–46 | Position Tile, Lineage and multiple nominees | Coverage and hierarchy |
| 46–58 | Matrix Grid and talent review | Calibration evidence |
| 58–70 | AI-assisted recommendation and explanation | Human accept/reject |
| 70–80 | Development action and learning link | Skill gaps and milestones |
| 80–88 | Vacancy and emergency cover | Continuity scenario |
| 88–96 | Security, reporting and manager transfer | Negative access test and audit |
| 96–100 | Migration, TCO and decisions | Written assumptions |

## Challenge cases
Manager attempts to view another department's confidential nominees; candidate nominated to two positions; incumbent changes; AI recommends unsuitable person; committee revises readiness; critical position has zero ready-now successors; external candidate is considered with appropriate consent.

**Demo scoring:** continuity and nomination 30%; architecture 20%; privacy/fairness 20%; development and analytics 20%; operational/TCO 10%.

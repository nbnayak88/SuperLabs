## Suggested thresholds
100% confidential access and identity tests pass; zero AI-only nomination/selection; 100% critical-role coverage metrics reconciled to test records; 100% integration errors observable; ≥95% noncritical scripted steps without workaround; human talent committee signs off on readiness and fairness controls.

## Decision gates
G1 discovery → G2 position data and security → G3 vendor scripted demo → G4 POC and RFI scoring → G5 five-year TCO → G6 executive recommendation.

## Decision memo
Critical-role risks | SAP reference | shortlisted platforms | analyst evidence limits | weighted RFI | POC | fairness/security | integration complexity | TCO | decision | approvers.

## Learning experiment
Fictional industrial organization: the operations director retires in 9 months. Learners simulate CHRO, HRBP, line manager and security reviewer; identify successors, expose skill gaps, compare emergency coverage and justify a human-approved succession plan.

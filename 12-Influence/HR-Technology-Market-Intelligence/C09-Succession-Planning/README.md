## Purpose
Secure continuity for critical positions through nominated successors, readiness assessment, talent pools, calibrated talent review, development actions and confidential governance.

**Boundary:** C09 is succession coverage; C10 is broader talent intelligence/internal mobility; C11 is leadership development/coaching; C07 is formal performance reviews.

**Governance:** No algorithmically final succession decision. Readiness is a reviewed planning judgment, not a promise of promotion. Protect sensitive talent flags and nominations.

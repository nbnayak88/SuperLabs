# C07 Market and Analyst Evidence

## Market definition
Performance management includes goal alignment, performance reviews, continuous conversations, competencies, calibration, feedback, employee acknowledgment and development planning. Adjacent markets include HCM suites, talent management suites, employee engagement and OKR management.

## Evaluation rules
Test each product's current release, country support, form customization, ratings, calibration, development integration, AI entitlements and data export. A vendor with strong OKRs is not automatically superior in formal performance appraisal. Separate native functions from partner features and customizations.

## Presales differentiation
- SAP: effective-dated Employee Central context, goal/competency structures, performance forms, calibration and integration with compensation/development.
- Workday/Oracle: compare HCM suite consistency and cross-module integration effort.
- Lattice/15Five/Culture Amp: compare continuous coaching and employee experience.
- Betterworks: compare enterprise goal alignment and performance dialogue.
- Leapsome/PerformYard: compare flexible cycles, usability and reporting.

# C07 — 100-Minute Demonstration
**Personas:** employee, line manager, HRBP, calibration facilitator, CHRO, compensation lead, SAP architect and privacy officer.

| Minutes | Scenario | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Strategy and SAP architecture | Scope, entitlements and fit |
| 10–22 | Goal creation and alignment | Goal library, approval and weights |
| 22–34 | Continuous check-in and feedback | Notes, coaching and permissions |
| 34–46 | Self-assessment and manager review | Competencies, rating scale, form route |
| 46–58 | Manager change and mid-cycle transfer | Effective-date handling and history |
| 58–70 | Calibration with HR governance | Ratings, rationale and audit |
| 70–80 | AI-assisted feedback drafting | Human correction, no auto-rating |
| 80–88 | Development and compensation handoff | Approved result and data lineage |
| 88–96 | Security negative test and analytics | Access denial, completion, ratings |
| 96–100 | Implementation and commercial assumptions | Written TCO and gaps |

## Required live challenges
1. Manager cannot view a former employee's restricted review after transfer unless policy allows.
2. Employee on leave receives correct cycle treatment.
3. AI invents an achievement; manager rejects and edits.
4. Calibration changes rating; record original, rationale and approver.
5. Compensation interface fails; retry without duplicate results.
6. Employee disputes evaluation; acknowledgment and appeal are recorded.

Demo score: business fit 30%; architecture 20%; fairness/privacy 20%; employee/manager experience 15%; operational evidence 15%.

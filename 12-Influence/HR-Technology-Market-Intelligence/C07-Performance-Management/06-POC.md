## Proposed decision thresholds
100% mandatory security, privacy, identity, calibration integrity and human-decision tests pass; zero unauthorized review disclosures; 100% integration failures detectable; 100% approved compensation outputs reconciled in test; ≥95% noncritical steps completed without workaround; fairness and appeals governance signed by HR/legal.

## Gates
G1 discovery and policy → G2 architecture and privacy → G3 common vendor demo → G4 POC and RFI scoring → G5 five-year TCO → G6 executive recommendation.

## Decision memo
Business objectives | SAP reference | Vendor longlist | Analyst evidence | RFI score | POC pass/fail | Fairness/privacy | Integration risk | TCO | Recommendation | Conditions | Approvers.

## Learning experiment
Run a fictional performance cycle with manager, employee, HRBP and calibration panel personas. Compare annual review vs continuous coaching outcomes; evaluate decisions with documented evidence rather than rating averages alone.

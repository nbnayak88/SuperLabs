# C12 — 100-Minute Role-Based Demonstration

**Personas:** compensation lead, finance controller, HRBP, manager, employee, payroll lead, SAP architect and privacy officer.

| Minutes | Scene | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Scope and SAP architecture | SKU, data ownership and country coverage |
| 10–22 | Build salary plan and market ranges | Grade, range, currency |
| 22–34 | Allocate budgets and guidelines | Manager worksheet and budget controls |
| 34–46 | Merit planning and exceptions | Eligibility, approvals, audit |
| 46–58 | Variable pay and performance factors | Calculation and source lineage |
| 58–70 | Pay equity and compression review | Controlled analytics and methodology |
| 70–80 | Multi-currency and effective-dated changes | Currency and payroll rules |
| 80–88 | AI-assisted scenario with human approval | Unsupported recommendation rejected |
| 88–96 | Security and downstream reconciliation | Access negative test, retry |
| 96–100 | Five-year TCO and decision | Evidence register |

## Live challenges
Manager exceeds budget; employee changes FTE mid-cycle; two currencies use different rate dates; AI invents performance evidence; HRBP tries to view unauthorized salary; payroll receives duplicate event; approved pay adjustment must reconcile to Employee Central.

Demo scoring: planning and bonus 30%; integration 20%; confidentiality and fairness 25%; analytics and UX 15%; commercial/operations 10%.

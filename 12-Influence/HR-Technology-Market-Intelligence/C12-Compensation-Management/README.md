## Outcomes
Govern merit and bonus cycles, ensure budget integrity, improve compensation decisions, strengthen pay equity controls and reconcile approved outcomes with core HR and payroll.

## Scope boundary
C12 includes salary planning, merit, bonus/variable pay, market-pricing inputs, salary structures and total rewards. C14 focuses on recognition. C18–C20 cover payroll execution and statutory compliance. Sales commission engines may require a separate specialist evaluation.

## Governance
Compensation recommendations are human approved; market survey data licensing, confidentiality, fairness, transparency and country-specific rules must be validated. No invented analyst rankings or unverified native SAP parity.

# C12 — Market and Analyst Evidence

## Market segments
1. Enterprise compensation planning: merit, salary review, promotions, budgets, approvals and variable pay.
2. Compensation intelligence: salary survey matching, market pricing, ranges, benchmarking and pay equity.
3. Specialist incentives: complex bonus/commission plans and calculation engines.

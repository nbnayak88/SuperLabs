# C06 — Analyst Research and Competitive Landscape

## Market segmentation
1. **Talent assessment:** psychometric, cognitive, personality, situational judgment, language, technical and job simulations.
2. **Interview intelligence:** structured question preparation, scheduling, scorecards, recording/transcription where consented, feedback summaries and interviewer coaching.
3. **Decision intelligence:** evidence synthesis and calibration to assist accountable humans, not automate hiring outcomes.

## Evaluation caveats
- SAP AI question generation and feedback insights are not a substitute for validated psychometric assessment.
- The specialist products have different scopes; compare by use case rather than asserting feature equivalence.
- SAP Help documents a third-party assessment integration pattern involving SHL via Boomi, but implementation support and current contractual status must be validated.
- Product-specific 2026 rankings and pricing remain unverified until sourced.

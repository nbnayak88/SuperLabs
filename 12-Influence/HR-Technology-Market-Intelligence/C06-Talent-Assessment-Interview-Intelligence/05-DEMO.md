# C06 — 100-Minute Demonstration Script

| Time | Demonstration | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Scope, SAP recruiting architecture, feature entitlements | Product boundaries and licenses |
| 10–20 | Role competency and structured scorecard | Job-related rubric |
| 20–32 | AI-assisted interview question draft | Human review and rejected unsafe question |
| 32–44 | Candidate scheduling and accessibility | Consent and accommodation |
| 44–58 | Structured human-led interview | Independent panel evidence |
| 58–70 | Specialist assessment / coding test | Validity and secure candidate link |
| 70–80 | Assessment result integration | Provenance, retries and ATS correlation |
| 80–88 | AI feedback synthesis and human correction | Audit trail, no auto-rejection |
| 88–96 | Fairness, security and recording opt-out | Negative access and consent tests |
| 96–100 | Five-year TCO and vendor evidence | Assumptions and unresolved gaps |

## Required live challenges
- AI suggests a discriminatory or irrelevant question; interviewer rejects it.
- Candidate opts out of recording; interview still proceeds.
- Applicant needs an accessible alternate assessment.
- Two interviewers disagree; independent evidence remains intact.
- Assessment API fails and is retried without duplicate results.
- Unauthorized interviewer requests another candidate's results; access denied.
- AI summary misstates an answer; human corrects it with preserved provenance.

**Demo scoring:** assessment validity 25%; human-led governance 25%; integration 20%; candidate/interviewer experience 15%; privacy/security 15%.

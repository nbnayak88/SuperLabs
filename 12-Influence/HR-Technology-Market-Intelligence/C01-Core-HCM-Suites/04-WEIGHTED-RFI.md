## Must-pass gates independent of weighted score

1. Mandatory jurisdictional/privacy controls.
2. No unauthorized sensitive-data access.
3. Accurate employee effective-dated history.
4. Recoverable integration failure without silent loss.
5. Demonstrated legal-entity/country-specific process fit.
6. Auditable migration and reconciliation.

## Evaluation template

Vendor | Requirement ID | Score 0–5 | Weighted points | Evidence URL | Exception | Remediation | Evaluator | Date.

## Commercial cost model

Include subscription by employee band, modules, sandbox/nonproduction, implementation, localization, integration, migration, support, premium AI, training, ongoing release testing and exit/migration costs over five years. Keep commercial and technical assumptions explicit.

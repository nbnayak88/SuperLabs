# C01 Market Definition, Analyst Evidence and Competitive Landscape

## Market definition

Core HCM is the authoritative cloud platform for employee and employment records, organizational relationships, HR transactions and employee/manager services. A full HCM suite includes adjacent talent, payroll, workforce management and analytics capabilities; assess them in their own C-categories.

## Competitive objection handling

- **“Workday/Oracle have one suite.”** Request evidence of cross-module data ownership, workflow lineage, localization and integration effort, rather than relying on suite branding.
- **“SAP is complex.”** Compare a bounded EC configuration using the same synthetic population and measurable transaction paths; document extensions and change governance.
- **“Dayforce/UKG integrate time and payroll.”** Assess global statutory footprint and payroll/time scope in C18/C22, while verifying C01 integration contracts.
- **“Darwinbox is faster.”** Run equivalent implementation and migration assumptions, languages, country requirements and security gates.
- **“AI will replace HR operations.”** Require human approval, auditable suggestions, access controls, bias evaluation and fallback.

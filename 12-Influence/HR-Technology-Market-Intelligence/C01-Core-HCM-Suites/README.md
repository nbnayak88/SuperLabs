## Business outcomes

Create a trusted, effective-dated global employee and organizational system of record; reduce HR transactions and errors; enable employee/manager self-service; integrate payroll, time, identity and downstream business systems; govern privacy and access; support acquisitions and multi-country operations.

## Scope boundaries

C01 covers core employee records, effective-dated employment and organizational structures, self-service, HR workflows, foundational data governance and enterprise connectivity. Dedicated recruiting (C04), payroll (C18), time (C22), EXM (C27) and workplace (C03) capabilities are assessed separately but their interfaces are in scope.

## Evidence rule

Gartner, Forrester and IDC publications establish relevant market coverage; they do **not** establish a fabricated cross-analyst ranking. The ten vendors are an evaluation universe, not a ranking. Product coverage, licensing and country support must be demonstrated.

## Engagement deliverables

Discovery decisions; requirement traceability; current/target architecture; data model and integration inventory; signed POC scorecard; five-year TCO; executive selection memo; and open-risk register.

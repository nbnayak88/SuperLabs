# C11 — Market and Competitive Evidence

## Market segmentation
Leadership learning and development platforms; digital coaching networks; coaching management and matching; leadership assessment and behavior-change measurement; mentoring and cohort experiences.

## Analyst research discipline
Gartner, Forrester and IDC publish adjacent learning, talent management and coaching-related research, but a current dedicated C11 analyst ranking and exact vendor universe have **not been independently verified** for this pack. Do not invent Magic Quadrant, Wave or MarketScape placements. Request licensed reports and record exact title, publication date and evaluated-vendor inclusion before assigning analyst-derived scores.

## Evidence requests
Coach credentials and vetting; coverage by country and language; coach matching and rematch; data separation; confidentiality agreement; session retention; behavioral measurement methodology; 360 validity; SSO and SAP integration; AI policy; participant accessibility; five-year TCO; exit and portability.

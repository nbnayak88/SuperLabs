## Business outcomes
Leadership capability growth, coaching access, stronger succession readiness, observable behavior change and measurable participation without compromising coaching confidentiality.

## Category boundaries
C11 covers leadership development journeys, coaching, mentor matching, cohort programs, assessments and outcomes. C09 owns succession nominations; C15 owns enterprise LMS; C16 owns learning experience platforms.

## Key distinction
SAP supports learning/development planning, not automatically full specialist coaching functionality. Coach networks, matching, session notes, 360 instruments and outcome analytics require product- and contract-specific validation.

## Human-centered governance
Coaching session content is confidential by default subject to agreed legal and safety exceptions. Employer dashboards must not expose private notes or transcripts. AI does not replace qualified professional judgment.

# C11 — 100-Minute Demonstration

**Personas:** HR leadership development head, executive participant, coach, line manager, learning administrator, SAP architect, privacy officer and procurement.

| Minutes | Scene | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Business outcomes and SAP/partner architecture | Scope, SKUs and ownership |
| 10–22 | Identify leadership gap and development plan | Competency mapping |
| 22–34 | Assign SAP Learning pathway | Curriculum and enrollment |
| 34–46 | Match leader to qualified coach | Matching, choice and rematch |
| 46–58 | Book session and set participant-owned goal | Scheduling and consent |
| 58–70 | Run coaching workflow and confidentiality tests | Access boundaries |
| 70–80 | Assessment, feedback and development progress | Validity and evidence |
| 80–88 | AI assistance with human review | Safe prompt and escalation |
| 88–96 | Aggregate outcomes and SAP integration replay | Privacy and data lineage |
| 96–100 | Commercial and implementation review | Five-year TCO |

## Required challenges
HR tries to open coach notes; participant rejects assigned coach; employee withdraws consent; manager requests private transcript; AI generates harmful or unsupported feedback; integration retries enrollment; aggregated results risk reidentifying a small cohort.

Demo scoring: coaching quality 25%; confidentiality 25%; SAP architecture 20%; outcome measurement 20%; UX/commercial 10%.

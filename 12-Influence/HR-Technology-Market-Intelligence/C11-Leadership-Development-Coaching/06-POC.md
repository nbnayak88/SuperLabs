## Suggested gates
100% confidentiality, consent, permissions and identity scenarios pass; zero unauthorized session-note disclosure; 100% integration failures observable and safely replayable; ≥95% noncritical workflow steps without workaround; qualified-coach and accessibility evidence approved; no AI-only consequential HR decisions.

## Decision gates
G1 discovery → G2 privacy and coaching governance → G3 SAP/native-vs-partner architecture → G4 scripted demo → G5 POC and weighted RFI → G6 five-year TCO → G7 selection.

## Decision record
Leadership objectives | SAP baseline | vendor shortlist | analyst evidence limitations | coaching credentials | privacy architecture | RFI | POC | outcome methodology | five-year TCO | recommendation | approvals.

## SuccessLabs experiential learning
Simulate an emerging leader, coach, line manager, HR program owner and privacy reviewer. Contrast what the participant may share voluntarily with what an employer may legitimately collect; evaluate program value using observable behavior changes rather than private coaching disclosures.

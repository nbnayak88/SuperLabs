## Mandatory pass gates
Candidate privacy and lawful processing; no unauthorized candidate-data exposure; human accountability for hiring decisions; accessible candidate journey; accurate offer-to-EC handoff; recoverable integration errors; auditable selection decisions.

## RFI evidence workbook schema
Vendor | SAP path/edition | Requirement ID | Score 0–5 | Weighted score | Evidence URL | Demo timestamp | Country | Exception | Remediation | Reviewer | Date.

## Financial model
Five-year normalized TCO = subscription + implementation + ATS/CRM/AI entitlements + integration + assessments + migration + localization + training + support + upgrades + exit costs. Do not assume SmartRecruiters and legacy SF Recruiting licensing are interchangeable.

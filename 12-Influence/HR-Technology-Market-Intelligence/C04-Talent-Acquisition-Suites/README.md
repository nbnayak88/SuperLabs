# C04 — Talent Acquisition (Recruiting) Suites | Presales Pack v1.0

**HR stream:** HR02 Talent Acquisition | **Taxonomy:** C04 | **Research date:** 20 November 2026

## SAP-first reference
**SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting**, with **SmartRecruiters for SAP SuccessFactors** as an additional SAP portfolio path requiring distinct solution, licensing and migration evaluation. SAP SuccessFactors Recruiting Marketing and Onboarding are adjacent components subject to scope and entitlement. Do **not** count SmartRecruiters as an independent SAP competitor in the 2026 competitive longlist.

## Core outcomes
Faster approved requisitions, accessible candidate journeys, effective sourcing, consistent interviewer evaluation, controlled offers, compliant candidate data, and clean new-hire handoff into Employee Central.

## Scope boundary
C04: requisitions, posting, ATS, candidate communications, selection, interviews, offers, onboarding handoff, reporting, suite-level CRM/AI. Specialist recruitment marketing belongs to C05; assessment and interview intelligence to C06. Cross-category capabilities are tested as interfaces without duplicate taxonomy IDs.

## Evidence policy
A Gartner MQ inclusion is **not** a vendor rank. Gartner Peer Insights Customers' Choice is review-derived, **not** an MQ placement. Product claims must be demonstrated for the exact SAP product, release, country, licensing and integration configuration.

## Human-led AI principle
AI may draft, summarize and suggest; people approve requisitions, evaluate candidates, determine shortlists and make hiring decisions. Never automatically reject candidates based solely on opaque AI outputs.

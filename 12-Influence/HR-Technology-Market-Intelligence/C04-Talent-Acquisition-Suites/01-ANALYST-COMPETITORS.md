## Competitive hypotheses
- SAP: test EC data ownership, position/requisition linkage, hiring-to-employee handoff and total integration complexity.
- Workday/Oracle: test benefits of HCM-native deployment vs SAP-centric enterprise landscape.
- iCIMS/Greenhouse: test specialist ATS workflows, recruiter productivity and interview quality.
- Avature/Phenom: test candidate CRM, campaign attribution and talent pools.
- Eightfold: test skills explainability, consent, fairness and AI oversight.
- Darwinbox/Cornerstone: test regional localization and depth of ATS, onboarding and integrations.

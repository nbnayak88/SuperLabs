# C10 — Market, Analyst Evidence and Competitors

## Capability segments
1. Skills intelligence: skill ontology, extraction/inference, proficiency, validation, supply-demand analysis.
2. Talent marketplace: personalized internal jobs, gigs, projects, mentoring and learning.
3. Internal mobility: eligibility, manager approval, recruiting handoff, transfer and career pathways.

## Important differentiation
A specialist skills engine may infer capabilities without offering end-to-end job/gig mobility. A marketplace may recommend opportunities without a fully governed skills ontology. Score both layers separately; verify exact tenant availability.

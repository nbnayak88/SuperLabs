## Proposed acceptance gates
100% identity/privacy/access tests pass; zero AI-only mobility decisions; 100% skill corrections reflected in test outputs; 100% failed integrations detected and safely replayed; ≥95% noncritical scenarios without workaround; documented fairness review and employee agency.

## Selection stages
G1 discovery → G2 skills governance → G3 SAP path and integration architecture → G4 scripted vendor demos → G5 POC and RFI → G6 five-year TCO → G7 executive decision.

## Decision memo
Business outcome | SAP reference | shortlisted vendors | analyst evidence limitations | weighted RFI | POC | skills governance | fairness/privacy | integration risk | TCO | recommendation.

## SuccessLabs role-play
Employee, line manager, internal recruiter, skills steward and AI architect simulate a gig-mobility journey. Evaluate whether evidence-based matching improves opportunity access while preserving employee choice and fair treatment.

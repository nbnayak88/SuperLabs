## SAP-first reference
SAP SuccessFactors Talent Intelligence Hub (TIH), Growth Portfolio and Opportunity Marketplace, integrated with Employee Central, Learning, Recruiting and Succession & Development where supported, configured and licensed.

## Objective
Make workforce skills visible and actionable; enable transparent internal jobs, projects, gigs, mentoring and learning opportunities; improve mobility, skill growth and retention without opaque algorithmic decisions.

## Scope boundary
C10: skills intelligence used for talent discovery, matching, opportunity marketplaces, career pathways and internal mobility. C17: enterprise-wide skills intelligence as a standalone technology category. C09: formal succession planning. C06: candidate assessment. C15–C16: learning systems.

## Governance
Skills inferences are hypotheses, not facts. Employees can inspect and correct profile data; authorized humans approve consequential mobility decisions. No unsupported analyst vendor rankings.

## Live challenges
Incorrect AI-inferred skill; employee opts out of inference; two employees with different digital histories; manager attempts to block mobility without approved policy; confidential succession flag appears in marketplace (must be prevented); Recruiting API fails; skills data becomes stale.

Demo scoring: skills intelligence 25%; marketplace and mobility 30%; SAP integration 20%; fairness/privacy 15%; usability/TCO 10%.

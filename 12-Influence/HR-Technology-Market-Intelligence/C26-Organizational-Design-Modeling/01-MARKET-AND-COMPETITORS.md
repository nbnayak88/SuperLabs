## SAP 2H 2026 change
[SAP Learning](https://learning.sap.com/courses/sap-successfactors-employee-central-core-and-position-management/describing-key-sap-successfactors-employee-central-core-and-position-management-2h-2026-features-and-enhancements_a27e0fef-d71b-4aa0-81e7-21b407aff366) documents projects, simulations and publication of supported changes. [SAP Community introduction](https://community.sap.com/t5/human-capital-management-blog-posts-by-sap/introducing-organizational-modeling-in-sap-successfactors-employee-central/bc-p/14503306) identifies legal entity, business unit, division, department, cost center and position as supported object types; operations vary by object. It describes provisioning requests and a Position Management foundation with position-to-job synchronization. Verify tenant availability before promising functionality.

Other candidates require exact SKU and supported-operation evidence; this longlist includes complementary and manual alternatives, not ten equivalent products. No dedicated Gartner/IDC/Forrester ranking independently verified. Require report title/date/scope and primary product proof.

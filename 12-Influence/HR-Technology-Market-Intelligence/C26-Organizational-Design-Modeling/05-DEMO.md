# 100-minute role-based demo

Personas: CEO, CHRO, CFO, organization design lead, HRIS architect, employee representative and change lead.

| Minutes | Scenario | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Strategy and SAP 2H 2026 baseline | Scope/enablement |
| 10–22 | Current hierarchy and positions | Source reconciliation |
| 22–34 | Design scenario A | Isolated changes |
| 34–46 | Design scenario B | Alternative operating model |
| 46–58 | Spans, layers and vacancies | Metric trace |
| 58–70 | Cost, capabilities and transition impact | Financial evidence |
| 70–80 | Review and approved test publication | Supported operations |
| 80–88 | Permissions and consultation | Negative tests |
| 88–96 | Downstream reconciliation and failure handling | Recovery evidence |
| 96–100 | TCO and decision | Trade-offs |

Challenge: merge two departments while preserving regulated-role accountability, valid reporting lines and cost centers. Score modeling 30%, SAP integration 25%, analytics 20%, governance 15%, TCO 10%.

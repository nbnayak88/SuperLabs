## Suggested acceptance
100% metric integrity, confidentiality, approval and audit tests pass; 100% integration failures detected; zero invented AI KPI values; 100% source-to-dashboard lineage for test KRs; ≥95% noncritical steps complete without workaround; documented distinction between individual performance goals and team OKRs.

## Decision gates
G1 discovery and OKR policy → G2 SAP/native-vs-specialist architecture → G3 vendor demo → G4 POC and weighted RFI → G5 five-year TCO → G6 executive selection.

## Decision record
Strategy outcomes | SAP reference | Shortlist | Analyst evidence | Weighted RFI | POC | KPI governance | Security | Integration complexity | TCO | Recommendation | Approvals.

## SuccessLabs learning experiment
Simulate quarterly OKR planning, weekly check-ins and retrospective with strategy owner, team lead, analyst and HRBP personas. Ask learners to distinguish activity metrics from genuine outcomes and defend a buy-vs-build decision using evidence.

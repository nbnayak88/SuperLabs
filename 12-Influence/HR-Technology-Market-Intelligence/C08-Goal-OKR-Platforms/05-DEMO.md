# C08 — 100-Minute Presales Demo

| Minutes | Scenario | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Business strategy and platform boundaries | SAP path, licensing, operating model |
| 10–22 | Create enterprise objective and 3 KRs | Baselines, targets and owners |
| 22–34 | Align business-unit and team OKRs | Cross-team links, no forced cascade |
| 34–46 | Connect governed SAP KPI actuals | Source lineage, refresh and reconciliation |
| 46–58 | Weekly check-in and confidence | Blockers, comments, history |
| 58–70 | Strategy map and dependency escalation | Executive portfolio view |
| 70–80 | AI-assisted recommendations | Human review and source-grounding |
| 80–88 | SuccessFactors individual goal boundary | HR goal mapping without forced rating |
| 88–96 | Access controls, analytics and failed API replay | Negative tests and audit |
| 96–100 | Commercial assumptions and transition | TCO, export, implementation |

## Live challenges
1. Two teams share an objective but own different KRs.
2. KPI source fails; dashboard shows stale data instead of invented progress.
3. Executive changes a target; preserve approval and previous target.
4. AI claims 100% progress without source; reject the claim.
5. Manager attempts to convert stretch OKR attainment into automatic appraisal rating; block this policy anti-pattern.
6. Restricted strategy objective is invisible to unauthorized staff.

Demo score: strategy execution 30%; metric/data architecture 25%; governance/security 20%; UX/adoption 15%; implementation/TCO 10%.

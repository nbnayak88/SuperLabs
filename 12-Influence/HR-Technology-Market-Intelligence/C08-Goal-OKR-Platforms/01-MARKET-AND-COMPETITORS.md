# C08 — Market and Analyst Evidence

## Market definition
Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) platforms translate strategy into measurable objectives and outcomes, connect teams and initiatives, support recurring check-ins and show execution risk. They overlap performance management, strategy execution, enterprise planning and project/portfolio management.

## Evidence requests
Product demo, roadmap, support and SLA, current SKU, enterprise references, SAP integration pattern, metric lineage, export rights, 5-year TCO, security certifications and geographic availability.

## Decision hypotheses
- SAP: strongest fit when employee goals and SuccessFactors HR context dominate; validate strategy execution depth separately.
- Betterworks: compare performance and goal alignment.
- Quantive/WorkBoard: compare strategy execution, enterprise hierarchy and dependency visualization.
- Profit.co/Perdoo/Mooncamp: compare operational OKR usability, adoption and cost.
- Lattice/Leapsome: compare goal alignment in employee performance workflows.

## Research discipline
Never label this internal C08 taxonomy as a Gartner/IDC/Forrester official market category; use exact published analyst titles when licensed evidence becomes available.

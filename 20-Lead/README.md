# 20 — Lead Lab

**SuccessLabs Academy SuperLabs** | *Architecting Experiences for a Better World*

## Purpose

Practice strategy, leadership, culture and executive decision making.

## Applicability

Applicable across 12 architecture streams, 11 GICS sectors, SAP and non-SAP platforms, and learning levels from Pre-Foundation to Ultimate. Core mechanics are domain-agnostic, with interchangeable industry and platform content packs.

## Learning-to-delivery journey

1. **Discover:** frame a leadership challenge and stakeholder priorities.
2. **Experiment:** explore alternative strategies and trade-offs.
3. **Practice:** simulate executive decisions and organizational change.
4. **Validate:** evaluate evidence, ethics, risks and measurable outcomes.
5. **Demonstrate:** present and defend a decision with a clear rationale.
6. **Reflect:** gather feedback and define leadership improvement actions.

## Future activity contract

Every exercise should document the challenge, level, prerequisites, duration, inputs, instructions, expected artifact, evaluation rubric, success measures and reflection prompts.

## Extension points

- `activities/` — hands-on leadership challenges
- `templates/` — ready-to-use strategy and leadership tools
- `simulations/` — executive and stakeholder role-play exercises
- `assessments/` — evidence-based rubrics
- `content-packs/` — interchangeable sector and technology contexts
- `examples/` — synthetic worked examples

## First candidate exercise

Run a fictional executive steering committee session on an enterprise transformation initiative. Produce a decision record and assess it against measurable criteria.

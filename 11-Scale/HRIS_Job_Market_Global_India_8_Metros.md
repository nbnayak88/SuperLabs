# HRIS vacancies on employer career sites and LinkedIn

**SuccessLabs Academy · Global examples and eight Indian metros · 28 November 2026 (IST)**

## What percentage of posted HRIS jobs requires SuccessFactors?

A reliable percentage for the world, India or any metro requires a dated, deduplicated set of actual HRIS requisitions. The public employer career pages and LinkedIn Jobs listings below establish where employers are recruiting and what they ask candidates to do. These examples were selected to cover platforms, cities and job types, so they do not establish a market share.

The unit of comparison is **one employer requisition requiring hands-on work in a named HRIS**. Count configuration, implementation, integrations, reporting, UAT, application support and operational ownership. Record the posting location and hiring employer. The posting links were surfaced during this review. Check the application button before applying because listings can close.

## 4. Recruiter playbook | Hire for ownership

| Lane | Titles to source | Interview prompt and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Implementation / consulting** | HCM Functional Consultant, EC Consultant, Payroll Lead, Solution Architect | “Walk me through a policy you turned into configuration.” Ask for design decisions, test cases, defects, cutover responsibilities and post-go-live evidence |
| **Technical / integration** | HCM Integration Consultant, Data Migration Analyst, HRIS Technical Analyst | “How did you reconcile an employee feed after a failed run?” Look for effective dates, mapping, duplicate handling, retries and source-to-target validation |
| **Application management / in-house HRIS** | HRIS Analyst, People Systems Analyst, HR Technology Specialist, Application Support Analyst | “A release changed absence balances. How would you investigate?” Look for triage, audit trail, regression tests, stakeholder updates and monitoring |
| **Super user / operations** | HR Operations Analyst, Payroll Analyst, Talent Operations Specialist, Learning Administrator, HR Shared Services Analyst | “What did you own in the system?” Look for data corrections, reporting, UAT, exception handling, escalation and training. Confirm the role’s access limits |

Ask the hiring manager to identify the module, countries, project stage, deployment model, shift or travel expectation and target work location before sourcing. For GCCs supporting other countries, document the payroll geography separately from the employee’s India work location. Record whether each requisition is a new seat, a backfill or a contract whenever the employer provides that information.

## 5. Candidate playbook | An India hiring scenario

**Scenario:** An HR operations analyst in Hyderabad has used a cloud HRIS for employee records, time and monthly payroll inputs. They want to become a SuccessFactors consultant and can also consider Bengaluru or Pune. They start with **EC plus data, access and UAT**, then use payroll and time experience to pursue either an in-house HRIS role or an implementation support role.

1. **Search three lanes simultaneously.** Start with `"SuccessFactors" "Employee Central"`, `"SuccessFactors" "HRIS Analyst"`, `"SAP SF" "Application Support"` and `"HR Operations" "SuccessFactors"`. Use the past-week filter and search both the city and India-remote. For a second product, use `"Workday HCM" "People Systems"` or `"Oracle Fusion HCM" "Global HR"`; add a product where you can show hands-on work or a credible bridge.
2. **Build one portfolio case with synthetic data.** Map a transfer across legal entities, identify its effective-date and manager changes, specify role access, and show the downstream payroll and reporting checks. Include a mapping sheet, six UAT cases, one defect and retest, and a reconciliation summary. Label the work as a simulation. A candidate with tenant access can show permitted configuration evidence.
3. **Apply by responsibility.** For a super-user seat, show exception handling and UAT. For an analyst seat, show issue investigation, reporting and release validation. For a consulting seat, show requirements, configuration choices and cutover work at the level actually performed. A payroll specialist can run the same case for ECP, Oracle Payroll, Dayforce or UKG Time with different controls.
4. **Go straight to employers.** Check implementation partners, delivery centres and in-house HRIS teams. Use LinkedIn Jobs to discover requisitions. Verify the employer, location and application status on the company career page when available. For overseas remote postings, check country eligibility before applying.
5. **Track weekly conversion.** Record relevant ads, applications, recruiter calls and interviews by city and role lane. If super-user interviews materialise first, prioritise a role that gives access to UAT, ticket triage, reporting and releases. Those responsibilities build evidence for the next HRIS move.

**Interview response to adapt:** “I validated employee changes before payroll, reconciled exceptions with HR and payroll, and logged and retested defects during UAT. I can walk you through an effective-dated transfer and explain the checks I would run on manager access, payroll inputs and reporting.”

## 6. How to measure vacancy share from these two sources

1. Use the same 30-day posting window across a defined roster of employer career sites and LinkedIn Jobs. Record employer, job ID, posting date, metro, platform, role lane, direct link and LinkedIn link.
2. Match employer and requisition ID across sources. Where IDs are absent, compare employer, title, location and responsibilities. Count each requisition once, including reposts identified as duplicates.
3. Include all qualifying HRIS vacancies, including vendor-neutral ones. Tag products from job responsibilities. Count a multi-location role once nationally and once per eligible metro. Group Delhi, Noida and Gurugram as NCR and track unrestricted India-remote roles separately.
4. Report **unique vacancies requiring the platform ÷ all unique qualifying HRIS vacancies in the same geography and period × 100**. Publish denominators, coverage and separate results for implementation, technical consulting, in-house HRIS and super-user work. Multi-platform requirements can produce percentages whose sum exceeds 100%.

If a future 30-day NCR collection yields 18 SuccessFactors requisitions out of 60 qualifying HRIS requisitions, the measured share for that collection is 30%. This example uses illustrative counts.
